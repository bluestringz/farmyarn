1. Getting Started

Welcome to FarmYARN! When you first sign up, you'll spawn on your own private plot with a free Farmhouse already built. From here, you can farm, raise animals, decorate, cook, dress up, and visit your friends' farms — at your own pace, with friends if you'd like.

Your character

Your character walks around the world exactly like you'd expect — tap anywhere to walk there, tap an object to interact with it. Whatever tool you have selected on the bottom toolbar decides what tapping does.

Tip: If you're not sure what a button does, most tools show a small hint or toast message the first time you use them.

2. Moving Around

  • Walk: Tap any open tile — your character will actually walk there (not teleport).
  • Zoom/Pan: Pinch or scroll to zoom, drag to pan. Once you manually zoom, the game remembers your preference and won't snap back to a default view on its own.
  • Entering a building: Tap your house, Coop, Barn, or Mansion. Your character will actually walk up to the front door, the screen fades briefly, and you appear just inside. Leaving works the same way in reverse — you'll come back out right at the same door you used to go in.
  • The Casino (inside Central Park) works the same way — walk up to the door, fade in, and you're inside.

3. Farming Basics

Growing crops is the heart of the game. It's a simple four-step loop:

  1. Plow — turns grass into farmable soil. Select the Plow tool and tap a grass tile.
  2. Plant — select the Plant tool, which opens the Seed Picker so you can choose which seed to plant and see how many you own. Tap a plowed tile to plant. You'll need to buy seeds from the Shop first if you don't have any.
  3. Water — crops grow properly once watered at least once. Select Water and tap (or hold and drag across several tiles at once — no need to tap one at a time).
  4. Harvest — once a crop finishes growing, select Harvest and tap it to collect the yield.
Don't neglect your crops! A crop left unwatered too long will die outright. A crop that's fully grown but left un-harvested too long will wither on the vine. Both are admin-configurable, but expect roughly half a day either way.

4. Energy & Resting

Plowing, planting, watering, and most other actions cost Energy. You can see your current Energy in the top bar.

  • Energy slowly regenerates over time on its own.
  • Eating food (cooked dishes or Park snacks) restores Energy instantly.
  • Sitting or lying down on a chair, bench, or bed makes Energy regenerate faster while you're resting. Tap the furniture to sit/lie down (your character will actually walk over to it first), and tap it again to get up.

5. Animals

Buy animals from the Shop's Animal tab, then place them either outdoors on the open farm or inside a Chicken Coop / Cow Barn's interior.

Feeding

Animals need feed, made from Wheat at the Silo (a building you must own). Select the Feed tool — this opens the Feed Picker, showing all four feed types and how many of each you're carrying. You must tap the correct feed type in the picker first, then tap the matching animal, or nothing will happen.

Collecting

Once fed, an animal produces something over time (eggs, wool, etc.) — tap it once ready to collect. Look for the green checkmark badge above it.

Animals can die of starvation if they go unfed for too long (about a day, admin-configurable). A small badge above the animal shows its feed status: orange with a "!" means it needs feeding, green with a "✓" means it's fed and just waiting on its timer.

6. Keeping Animals Warm (Fireplace & Bonfire)

Besides feeding, animals also need to stay warm at night:

  • Animals housed inside a Coop or Barn need a Fireplace placed somewhere in that same room.
  • Animals kept outdoors on the open farm need a Bonfire (a purchasable decoration) within a few tiles of them.

During the day, animals are always safe regardless of nearby heat. At night, an animal without a nearby heat source shows a small ❄️ blue snowflake badge above it — that's your warning. If it stays cold too long (about 4 hours, admin-configurable), it can die of cold. Bringing the heat source back in range (or waiting for daytime) resets the clock — a partial cold spell doesn't carry over.

Tip: One Fireplace covers the WHOLE room it's placed in — you don't need one per animal. One Bonfire outdoors covers every animal within its radius.

8. Interior Decorating

Step inside any of your buildings and use the Decorate tool to furnish the room — tables, chairs, beds, shelves, and more.

Wall-mounted décor

Items like the TV, Air Conditioner, Wall Light, and Paintings only make sense flush against a wall. They can only be placed along the top row, or the leftmost/rightmost column of the room — not the open bottom row facing the camera.

Tabletop décor

The Table Lamp and TV specifically have to sit on top of an actual Dining Table or Side Table — they can't just go on open floor.

Tip: A Table Lamp, Wall Light, or Fireplace also lights up the room at night — see Day, Night & Lighting.

9. The Closet & Costumes

Buying a costume in the Shop does NOT put it on. It only adds it to what you own — the Shop's Buy button just turns into "Owned" afterward.

To actually wear something, place a Closet (bought from the Shop's Interior tab) inside your house and tap it. It shows every costume you currently own, with a "Wear" button on each — that's the only place in the game that changes your look.

10. Special Outfits (GM Points)

Special Outfits (currently Lancer, Sorcerer, and Swordsman) are a rarer costume tier with their own animated magic aura that follows you around while worn. They're bought in the Shop's ⭐ Special tab, but unlike regular costumes:

  • They cost GM Points, never Premium Points — and GM Points can only be granted by an admin. There's no way to earn or buy them through normal play.
  • They're a 14-day rental (regular costumes are 7 days).
  • Just like regular costumes, buying one doesn't wear it — you still need the Closet.

11. Cooking & the Refrigerator

Place a Stove inside your house and tap it to cook harvested crops into food. Eating food restores Energy — see Energy & Resting.

The Refrigerator

A 2-Door Refrigerator (Shop → Interior) gives you cold storage specifically for ready-to-eat food — cooked dishes and Park snacks (Ice Cream, Hot Dog) — separate from your Bag. It does not accept raw crops/ingredients. Tap it to deposit or withdraw. When you eat, the game checks the Refrigerator first, then your Bag.

14. The Casino

Found inside Central Park (walk up to its door like any building). Three floors, three games:

FloorGameHow it works
1Color GamePick a bet amount, then a color. 3 blocks drop — match to win Energy.
2Claw MachineFixed bet, grab for a prize — Energy or occasionally Premium Points.
3Lucky 777Classic slot machine. Choose your bet, spin, match symbols. The rarest possible prize is landing "GM GM GM" — an actual GM Points reward.
Tip: The panels show possible rewards, not exact odds — bigger prizes are rarer, and betting more generally means a real shot at a bigger win, but also a lower chance of winning at all.

15. The Market

A shared plaza where every player's stall lives side by side. Rent a stall to sell items (including costumes you're not using — as long as their rental hasn't expired) to other players, or browse everyone else's stalls to buy.

18. Leaderboard

The 🏆 Most Rich panel (top-left of the screen) ranks the top 10 players by coins on hand. Top 3 get a medal (🥇🥈🥉) with special styling. Admin accounts never appear on it.

Note: The ranking only updates once a day, right at midnight (Philippine Time) — it won't shift in real time as people earn or spend coins throughout the day.

19. Day, Night & Lighting

The game follows real time — your farm and house both visibly darken at night. Placing light sources pushes the darkness back:

  • Outdoors: Lamp Post, Bonfire
  • Indoors: Table Lamp, Wall Light, Fireplace

These all have a gentle "breathing" glow and small glowing particles drifting around them, so your farm actually feels alive at night instead of just going dark.

20. Expanding Your Farm

Use the Expand button on the toolbar to grow your farm's size for a coin cost that increases with each expansion. A bigger farm means more room for crops, animals, and decorations.

21. Tips for New Players

  • Water your crops as soon as you plant them — an unwatered crop can die.
  • Build a Silo early if you plan to raise animals — you can't make feed without one.
  • Keep an eye on the ❄️ badge on your animals once you have any housed outdoors or without a Fireplace — a Bonfire is cheap insurance.
  • Don't forget the Closet — buying a costume is only half the job.
  • Rest (sit/lie down) instead of just waiting around when your Energy is low — it regenerates faster.
  • Check the Shop regularly — some items have limited stock and won't always be available.
